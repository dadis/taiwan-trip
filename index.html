Day 1 Nov 13 (Thu) • Taipei [Booked]
Arrival

Touchdown Taipei & First Night Bites

Land at Taoyuan (TPE), take the Airport Express MRT directly to Taipei Main Station, and check in.

Evening: Relaxed stroll through Ningxia Night Market. Try Liu Yu Zi fried taro balls & fresh oyster omelet.
Transit: Taoyuan Airport MRT Express (35 mins) — tap EasyCard.
Day 2 Nov 14 (Fri) • Taipei [Booked]
Urban & Sunset

Historic Shophouses & Skyline Dusk

Wander Dadaocheng / Dihua Street’s vintage tea courtyards, followed by sunset over the metropolis.

Morning: Dihua Street — heritage shophouses, herbal apothecaries, specialty oolong tea tasting.
Afternoon: Songshan Cultural & Creative Park + indie cafe hopping in Fujin Street.
Dusk: Elephant Mountain (easy 20-min stair climb for the iconic Taipei 101 skyline) + Raohe Night Market pepper buns.
Day 3 Nov 15 (Sat) • Taipei [Booked]
Tea Hills

Creative Hubs & Tea Plantation Cable Car

Explore converted sake factory galleries and ride a glass-floor gondola into misty tea mountains.

Morning: Huashan 1914 Creative Park & Yongkang Street (scallion pancakes, mango ice, boutique stationery).
Afternoon: Maokong Gondola up to mountain tea plantations. Enjoy cliffside Tieguanyin tea overlooking Taipei valley.
Evening: Shida student cafe & boutique quarter or quiet dinner in Daan.
Day 4 Nov 16 (Sun) • Hualien (Night 1)
Scenic Rail Leg 1

Pacific Coastal Rail & Pebble Beach

Board the express train cutting between towering cliffs and the azure Pacific Ocean.

Transit: TRA Express (EMU3000/Puyuma) from Taipei to Hualien (~2 hrs). Book in advance!
Afternoon: Check in, taxi or bike to Qixingtan Beach — relax listening to ocean waves rolling smooth marble pebbles.
Night: Dongdamen Night Market — wide open coastal market featuring indigenous roasted pork, scallion wraps, and lemon tea.
Day 5 Nov 17 (Mon) • Hualien (Night 2)
Coastal Calm

Cypress Groves & Oceanfront Cafes

Historic military quarters nestled under giant pine trees and leisurely Pacific cafe lounging.

Day: Visit Pine Garden (1940s Japanese military headquarter in a tranquil grove) + General Hall cultural quarters.
Food: Gongzheng steamed pork buns & legendary Dai Ji delicate wontons.
Afternoon: Relax at a seaside cafe along the coastal promenade.
Day 6 Nov 18 (Tue) • Taitung (Night 1)
Scenic Rail Leg 2

East Rift Valley Rail & Lantern Village

Train ride between the Central and Coastal mountain ranges into Taiwan’s most laid-back county.

Transit: TRA Valley Express to Taitung Station (~1.5–2 hrs).
Evening: Tiehua Music Village — stroll among hundreds of hand-painted glowing hot-air-balloon lanterns, acoustic live performances, and local craft booths.
Day 7 Nov 19 (Wed) • Taitung (Night 2)
🌾 Highlight

Chishang Golden Rice Field E-Biking

Rent electric bicycles and glide along Mr. Brown Avenue through boundless golden paddy fields.

Day Trip: 40-min easy train up to Chishang (no luggage to carry!).
Activity: Cycle through Mr. Brown Avenue (Bolang Dadao) with dramatic mountain backdrops. November is harvest time!
Lunch: Traditional Chishang Wooden Box Bento featuring award-winning valley rice and braised pork.
Day 8 Nov 20 (Thu) • Taitung (Night 3)
Bohemian Coast

Dulan Artist Village & Ocean Breeze

Bohemian indie art community, craft beer, ocean views, and indigenous culture.

Day: Coastal bus or taxi to Dulan Sugar Factory (artist workshops, indie coffee, indigenous woodcraft).
Coast: Enjoy coastal view stops along Jiamuzi Bay and taste fresh Donghe steamed buns.
Day 9 Nov 21 (Fri) • Kaohsiung (Night 1)
Scenic Rail Leg 3

South-Link Train & Pier-2 Harbor Art

Cross the southern mountain pass to Taiwan’s bustling harbor city.

Transit: TRA South-Link Scenic Train (~2 hrs) to Kaohsiung Main Station.
Afternoon: Check in at Yancheng District. Walk the sprawling Pier-2 Art Center warehouses, giant murals, and rotating Great Harbor Bridge.
Dinner: Famous Yancheng Duck Rice (Ya Rou Zhen) or craft beer at KW2 harbor warehouse.
Day 10 Nov 22 (Sat) • Kaohsiung (Night 2)
Maritime Day

Cijin Island Ferry & Bay Light Rail

5-minute ferry hop to a barrier island for lighthouses, coastal breeze, and fresh seafood.

Morning: Ferry to Cijin Island. Walk up to the Kaohsiung Lighthouse cafe for panoramic harbor views, then sample grilled squid along Seafood Old Street.
Afternoon: Ride the circular Light Rail around the bay; stop at Weiwuying Street Art Village.
Evening: Lively Ruifeng Night Market (angel chicken cutlets, bubble tea).
Day 11 Nov 23 (Sun) • Tainan (Night 1)
Easy Transit Leg

Arrive in Taiwan's Culinary Heartland

Quick 30-min train to Taiwan's oldest city, famed for lantern-lit lanes and endless street delicacies.

Transit: Local TRA train from Kaohsiung to Tainan (~30 mins, no advance ticket needed, tap EasyCard).
Afternoon: Check into West Central District. Stroll Shennong Street — 300-year-old shophouses now housing cozy cafes, leathercraft, and cocktail bars.
Food Crawl: Guohua Street for savory rice pudding (*wa gui*) and Danzai noodles.
Day 12 Nov 24 (Mon) • Tainan (Night 2)
Heritage & Beef Soup

Banyan Treehouse & Legendary Beef Soup

Dutch colonial fortresses, banyans reclaiming old warehouses, and Michelin-starred breakfast bowls.

Breakfast: Traditional fresh Tainan beef soup (flash-boiled by pouring hot broth over fresh sliced local beef).
Morning: Anping Old Fort & Anping Treehouse (massive banyan aerial roots swallowing 19th-century brick warehouses).
Afternoon: Specialty coffee crawl through Tainan’s residential alleyways (Paripari apt.).
Day 13 Nov 25 (Tue) • Tainan (Night 3)
Design & Nightlife

Modern Art, Vintage Department Stores & Speakeasies

Stunning modern architecture, 1930s Japanese nostalgia, and hidden alleyway cocktail lounges.

Morning: Tainan Art Museum (Building 2, iconic white geometric design) + Hayashi Department Store (vintage dial elevator).
Afternoon: Sicao Green Tunnel mini-boat cruise through lush mangrove canopy.
Night: World-class alleyway speakeasies: Bar Home or Moonrock.
Day 14 Nov 26 (Wed) • Taipei Finale (Night 1)
High-Speed Rail

Bullet Train North & Chifeng Hipster Quarters

High-Speed Rail whisks you from south to north in just 85 minutes.

Transit: Taiwan High-Speed Rail (THSR) Tainan to Taipei (~85 mins, 300 km/h).
Afternoon: Check into Zhongshan District. Explore Chifeng Street — vintage auto-repair alleys now filled with design boutiques and specialty coffee roasters.
Night: Tonghua (Linjiang) Night Market for pan-fried pork buns and shaved ice.
Day 15 Nov 27 (Thu) • Taipei Finale (Night 2)
♨️ Thermal Day

Beitou Thermal Hot Springs & Tamsui River Sunset

Unwind in natural sulfur hot springs and watch sunset where the river meets the sea.

Morning: Red MRT Line to Beitou. Soak in private or public hot spring baths, visit Thermal Valley steam springs, and marvel at the wooden Beitou Eco-Library.
Late Afternoon: MRT to Tamsui riverfront promenade for golden sunset and iron eggs.
Dinner: Taiwanese Mala spicy hotpot feast.
Day 16 Nov 28 (Fri) • Taipei Finale (Night 3)
Souvenirs & Farewell

Traditional Breakfast & Gift Haul

Warm sesame flatbreads, high-mountain oolong tea shopping, and a farewell banquet.

Morning: Traditional savory soy milk & youtiao at Fuhang Soy Milk.
Day: Gift hunting: SunnyHills / Chia Te pineapple cakes, Taiwan craft tea tins, and design stationery.
Evening: Farewell dinner at Din Tai Fung (xiao long bao) or Taipei craft beer taproom.
Day 17 Nov 29 (Sat) • Departure
Flight Home

Smooth Airport Transit & Flight to Berlin

Direct Airport Express MRT from Taipei Main Station back to Taoyuan (TPE).

Transit: Taoyuan Airport MRT Express (35 mins). In-town check-in available at Taipei Main Station for select airlines!
🏙️

Taipei Part 1 (Nov 13–16, 3 Nights)

Status: Hotel Already Booked

Ideal base to unpack, shake off jetlag, explore Dadaocheng, Yongkang Street, and vibrant night markets.

🌊

Hualien (Nov 16–18, 2 Nights)

Recommended Area: Hualien City Center / Golden Triangle

Stay around Zhongshan Rd & Zhongzheng Rd. Walk out your door to dumplings, cafes, bakeries, and a 10-minute walk to Dongdamen Night Market.

💡 Alternative: Staying right beside Hualien Station is great if you want instant check-in straight off the train.
🌾

Taitung (Nov 18–21, 3 Nights)

Recommended Area: Tiehua Music Village / Old Railway Area

The cultural epicenter of Taitung. At night, acoustic singers and lantern markets fill the park.

💡 Day Trip Bonus: You can do the Chishang rice field bike day trip via a quick 40-min scenic train without relocating luggage!
⚓

Kaohsiung (Nov 21–23, 2 Nights)

Recommended Area: Yancheng District / Pier-2 Art Center

Trendy, walkable, harbor-side neighborhood. Steps from the light rail, Great Harbor Bridge, and the Cijin Island ferry terminal.

🍜

Tainan (Nov 23–26, 3 Nights)

Recommended Area: West Central District (Zhongxi)

Tainan has no subway system, so location is critical! Staying near Shennong Street, Chihkan Tower, or Tainan Art Museum puts 90% of food stalls and indie cafes within walking distance.

🛍️

Taipei Finale (Nov 26–29, 3 Nights)

Recommended Area: Zhongshan District / Chifeng Street

Taipei’s premier cafe and design boutique quarter. Direct access to the Red MRT Line (for Beitou Hot Springs & Taipei 101) and just 1 stop from the Taoyuan Airport Express MRT.

⚠️

TRA Express Booking Rule (Crucial)

On the Eastern Line (Taipei ↔ Hualien ↔ Taitung), the fastest trains (EMU3000, Puyuma, and Taroko Express) are Reserved Seat Only.

No Standing / No EasyCard Tapping: You cannot simply swipe an EasyCard to board these express trains. You must have an assigned seat ticket booked in advance.
Booking Window: Tickets open 28 days prior at 00:00 Taiwan time (UTC+8).
Official App: Download "Taiwan Railway (台鐵e聯網)". You can book in English with your passport number and get digital QR tickets directly on your phone.
